ActionBridge Ubuntu companion — privacy

No advertising or analytics are included. Approved phones can send files,
clipboard text, links and print/open actions, and can retrieve items explicitly
queued for them. Pair only phones you trust; keep the QR and pairing code private.

Local connections use HTTPS and the Android app pins the computer certificate.
Internet connections use the existing ActionBridge Cloudflare signaling service
and encrypted direct WebRTC. The service receives connection metadata and
signaling; this version does not upload file contents to a central file store.
TURN relay is not enabled by this package. Restrictive networks can prevent
an internet connection; local transfers still work.

Pairing credentials, certificate, jobs and outgoing snapshots are stored in a
private user folder (~/.local/share/actionbridge, or XDG_DATA_HOME/actionbridge).
Received files are kept in Downloads/ActionBridge. Clipboard text can remain in
local action journals. Your operating system and printer spooler may retain data.
Do not share the private app folder. Protect it with your normal account security
and disk encryption. Uninstalling the app does not delete your files or pairing.

Use Devices to remove local phones or revoke remote access (internet required).
Closing the window keeps receiving; menu → Quit stops the companion. Login
startup is optional. No system-wide background daemon is installed.

Wayland may require an explicit Copy to clipboard click. CUPS queue acceptance
is shown as submitted; it does not establish that pages physically printed.
